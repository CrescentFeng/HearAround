# HearAround screenshot plan

Export 16:9 PNG files at 1600 × 900 or 1920 × 1080. Use the deployed HTTPS build, not design mockups.

1. **Hero and simple mode** — brand, product statement, privacy card, and the simple interaction area. No active alert required.
2. **Accessible real alert** — car-horn or siren result from a bundled licensed sample, with large icon, severity, short action, and `Real model` badge visible.
3. **Agent evidence** — technical mode showing model candidates, deterministic rule, Agent decision trail, and cooldown result.
4. **Evaluation and privacy** — holdout/stress evidence, browser capabilities, privacy statement, and experimental-product boundary.

Do not use the scenario preview as the main result screenshot. Do not crop out the source badge or safety disclaimer.
